How it works

Hand us a shoebox. Get the whole story back.

No mailing labels, no waiting on a warehouse. You meet the person who handles your tapes.

  1. Book a time

    Drop-offs are by appointment, Tuesday to Friday, 9 to 5, at our home office a few minutes up the hill behind Yokes in South Richland.

  2. We digitize and edit

    Every tape is converted 1 to 1 to USB or DVD. Step up a tier and the blank in-between footage is cut, chapters and custom titles are added, and the audio is cleaned up.

  3. Back in one week

    Because everything is handled locally, your tapes never ride in a truck. Choose Premium and we can also set up a private YouTube channel to share with family and friends.
